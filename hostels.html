Overview

Hostel Accommodation at EDC

The University maintains two hostels on campus — one for the male and one for the female students of the East Campus, Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University.

Hostel accommodation is available to regular full-time students of the University Schools of Studies at the East Delhi Campus — USAR, USDI, USAP and USMC. Seats are allotted every academic session as per the hostel allocation policy of EDC, and a mess facility is compulsory for all residents.

About Hostels

Hostel Life at the East Delhi Campus

The Hostels

The University maintains two hostels on campus, one for the male and one for the female students of the East Campus, Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University. Each of the hostels provides mess facilities for the residents.

It is the concerted effort of the University to provide a wholesome hostel facility for the residents and help them pursue their respective academic and professional goals to their best. It is a consistent endeavour of the hostel administration to follow the well-established ethos of hostel life and promote a healthy community atmosphere in the hostel.

Hostel Administration

The EDC Hostels are administered under a Chief Warden, East Delhi Campus Hostels, with two Wardens for each hostel supported by the hostel office. Rooms are allotted by the EDC hostel committee strictly as per the hostel allocation policy of EDC.

The day-to-day management of the hostel mess is done by the Residents' Committee under the overall supervision of the concerned Warden. The Hostel Welfare charge is at the disposal of the hostel wardens in consultation with the Student Welfare Committee.

View Wardens & hostel office

Zero tolerance: Ragging, violence, negative discrimination, and consumption or possession of alcoholic, tobacco products or narcotic substances are strictly prohibited in the hostels.

Students Are Expected to Bring

For hygienic reasons, every student is expected to bring their own:

Medical Care

  • Medical facilities are available on the University campus for residents during working hours; a first-aid box is available in the hostel office.
  • Any case of major illness or medical ailment should be reported to the Warden / hostel office.
  • Parents are required to inform the hostel about the physical and mental health of their ward at the time of admission, and of any noticeable changes thereafter.

Hostel Welfare

The Hostel Welfare charge is an integral component of hostel charges. It is used for the welfare of hostel residents, at the disposal of the hostel wardens in consultation with the Student Welfare Committee, and is subject to audit like other funds of the University.

Mess & Timings

Hostel Mess

The Hostel provides a compulsory mess facility for all residents. The day-to-day management of the mess is done by the Residents' Committee under the overall supervision of the concerned Warden. Meals are served on a self-service basis in the Dining Halls of the respective hostels.

Mess Rules

  • Mess facility is compulsory for all residents; no cooking is allowed inside hostel rooms.
  • No mess furniture, utensils or materials may be removed from the dining hall under any circumstances.
  • Meals are not allowed to be taken out of the dining hall. In exceptional cases of major illness, meals may be carried to the room with prior intimation to the hostel office.
  • A fine of Rs. 500/- is levied for each violation (as per the Hostel Admission Brochure 2026-27).
  • Food from outside and food delivery are not permitted inside the hostel premises.

Mess Charges & Mess-off

  • Monthly mess charges are displayed on the hostel notice board and adjusted against the annual mess fee at the end of the session; any deficit is recovered and any excess returned.
  • No mess-off is given during the academic session. Mess-off for 7 or more continuous days is permitted only during notified vacations, at Rs. 100/- per day, on application at the hostel office.
  • For fresh admissions, mess-off at Rs. 100/- per day is given from the first date of the month of admission to the day before admission.
  • Disputes regarding mess-off are resolved by the Hostel Wardens in consultation with the Mess Committee / Residents.
Admission & Rules

Admission, Hostel Charges & Regulations

Hostel admission is notified every academic session through the Hostel Admission Brochure on the University website. The summary below is taken from the brochures; the brochure for the relevant session is the authoritative document.

Eligibility & Allotment

  • Only students admitted as regular full-time students in the University Schools of Studies (USS) of the East Campus are eligible.
  • Allotment is made on an academic-session basis as per the academic calendar.
  • Admissions are made strictly as per University rules and the applicable reservation rules of the University and the Govt. of NCT of Delhi.
  • Students with benchmark disabilities (PwBD) receive first priority, followed by the other categories set out in the brochure.
  • A waiting list is prepared when seats are not available for all applicants.

How to Apply

  • Apply online through the hostel application form linked in the brochure for the session, and upload the required documents.
  • Submit the filled-in admission form with supporting documents at the hostel office at the time of physical allotment.
  • Allotment lists are published as notices. Filling the online form alone does not guarantee a hostel seat.
  • Parents / local guardians are requested to be present at the time of admission and re-admission.

Key Hostel Regulations

  • Last entry: 9:00 PM throughout the year; hostel entry and exit close at 9:00 PM. First exit time is 6:00 AM.
  • Attendance: taken daily between 9:00 and 9:30 PM. Residents with less than 75% aggregate attendance in the USS and hostel shall be denied residency in the next session.
  • Heaters, immersion rods, stoves, extra furniture and AC are not permitted in rooms.
  • Regular parking of motorised vehicles is not permitted to hostel residents.
  • Residents must stay in the room allotted at admission; unofficial room changes attract disciplinary action.

Visitors & Guests

  • Entry of day scholars or friends of residents is strictly prohibited.
  • Parents / local guardians / blood relatives may meet residents in the visitor room from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
  • Guests are not allowed in hostel rooms. Female guests in the Boys' Hostel and male guests in the Girls' Hostel are strictly prohibited.
  • Blood relatives may stay in the guest room only with the prior written permission of the Warden — at most four nights a month, at Rs. 400/- per night exclusive of meals — and not during end-term examinations.

Refund of Hostel Charges

  • A resident who vacates within two months of admission is refunded 50% of the hostel fee; otherwise the hostel fee is non-refundable.
  • Mess security and hostel security are refundable after adjustment of dues; mess charges are refunded after deduction of monthly bills.
  • Hostel security must be claimed within a year of leaving the hostel.
  • Mess maintenance and welfare fee are non-refundable.

Documents Required at Admission

  • Filled-in hostel application form and fee payment proofs
  • Mark sheets of 10th, 12th / equivalent / preceding semesters
  • Three recent passport-size photographs
  • Copy of admission slip and college I-Card
  • Medical certificate; Aadhaar copies of candidate and parent(s)
  • Local guardian's consent form; anti-ragging affidavit

The complete checklist and forms are in the annexures of the brochure.

About

About the East Campus Library

GGS Indraprastha University has developed a distinguished campus in East Delhi, including four academic institutions: the University School of Automation and Robotics (USAR); the University School of Design and Innovation (USDI); University School of Architecture & Planning (USAP) & University School of Mass Communication (USMC).

The central library at East Campus is established with the objective of the New National Education Policy (NEP), which strongly promotes (Science, Technology, Engineering, Architecture, Journalism), making diverse learning the foundation of every program in India. This approach aims to cultivate skills and knowledge in developing well-rounded individuals capable of providing informal and effective solutions to various problems.

The East Campus Library is located on the 5th and 6th floors of C-Block. The library has an average seating capacity of 125 pupils, are Wi-Fi equipped and have a designated area on the 5th floor for accessing digital materials.

The library operates using fully automated, cloud-based Integrated Library Management Software (Troodon) and is accessible to users from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday.

Web-OPAC

Web-OPAC — Online Library Catalogue

Gone are the days of searching a document in a library through card catalogue. OPAC and Web-OPAC changed the traditional card catalogue system.

A Web-OPAC is a library catalog on the web or Internet.

OPAC/Web-OPAC helps library user to search and retrieve the required bibliographic details of a documents in a library via terminals/Internet, without the assistance of a human intermediary, at any time and from any place of the world.

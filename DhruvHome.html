About Us

GGSIPU East Delhi Campus

A modern campus in Surajmal Vihar, Delhi — built for the Fourth Industrial Revolution, blending aesthetics, technology, and world-class learning.

The East Delhi Campus of Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University is committed to providing students with a world-class learning experience while fostering their holistic development. Designed to support emerging academic disciplines aligned with Industry 4.0, the campus champions interdisciplinary collaboration, research-driven pedagogy, and deep industry integration.

Four specialised schools — Automation & Robotics, Design & Innovation, Architecture & Planning, and Mass Communication.
Industry 4.0 curriculum spanning AI, robotics, IoT, design thinking, and media studies.
Research-driven pedagogy with modern laboratories, smart classrooms, and a net-zero energy vision.

A pioneering force in media education, research, and digital training. Established as the University Centre for Media Studies in 2006, it became a fully-fledged school in 2010 — a significant step in advancing mass communication studies.

Director (Academics), East Delhi Campus

Prof. Arvinder Kaur

Director (Academics), East Delhi Campus
Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University

Prof. Arvinder Kaur is a Professor at the University School of Information, Communication and Technology (USICT), GGSIPU, and holds additional charge as Director (Academics), East Delhi Campus. She also serves as Dean, University School of Automation & Robotics (USAR) and University School of Design & Innovation (USDI), and as Chairperson of the University's Staff Development Cell.

She holds a Ph.D. from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, an M.E. in Computer Engineering and a B.E. in Electrical Engineering, both from the Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala. She earlier served as Dean, USICT (2017–2020) and as faculty at the National Institute of Technology, Jalandhar (1993–2000).

Director In-charge (Administration), East Delhi Campus

Dr. S. P. Singh, IAS (Retd.)

Director In-charge (Administration), East Delhi Campus
Advisor cum Consultant, GGSIPU

Dr. S. P. Singh, IAS (Retd.), serves as Director In-charge (Administration) of the East Delhi Campus. As per the University's order of appointment, all files relating to the administrative functioning of the East Delhi Campus are routed through him to the higher authorities for administrative and financial approvals.

His responsibilities, as set out in the order, include supervising the functioning of non-teaching staff posted at the East Delhi Campus, preparing the action plan for the campus's administrative departments, developing and monitoring administrative policy within University rules, and coordinating among departments for cohesive functioning.

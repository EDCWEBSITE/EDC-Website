Activities of DSW

  • Financial assistance to students belonging to the Economically Weaker Section (EWS).
  • Merit-cum-Means linked financial assistance to meritorious and needy students.
  • Travel grant support for approved academic, cultural and sports participation.
  • Seminar grants, student welfare initiatives and academic support schemes.
  • Financial support for Freshers’ and Farewell functions of University Schools.
  • Annual cultural activities under Anugoonj and sports participation under University channels.
  • Formation, management and monitoring of student clubs under Srijan.

Campus Facilities & Student Engagement

East Delhi Campus encourages student participation in cultural, co-curricular and sports activities through structured clubs, student representation and campus-level welfare support.

  • Sports facilities for Football, Volleyball, Basketball, Cricket, Badminton, Kabaddi and Athletics.
  • Student clubs under Srijan for creativity, culture, social engagement and leadership.
  • Dedicated support and coordination through DSW office at East Delhi Campus.

Sports – East Delhi Campus

Sports & Physical Development

Sports and physical fitness remain a vital part of student life at East Delhi Campus. The campus supports participation in University-level sports activities and promotes active engagement across major team and individual sports.

Annual sports activities and inter-collegiate events are conducted under University coordination.

Major Sports Activities

  • Football, Volleyball, Basketball and Cricket
  • Badminton, Tennis, Kabaddi and Tug-of-War
  • Track & Field events including sprint and athletic events
  • Cross Country, Long Jump, Shot Put and Discus Throw

Training & Participation

  • Participation in University and approved inter-college competitions
  • Student representation through sports-related engagement and coordination
  • Encouragement of discipline, teamwork and campus sports culture

Student Discipline & Conduct

Proctor, East Delhi Campus

The Office of the Proctor is responsible for student discipline, conduct and campus decorum at the East Delhi Campus, and administers the Proctorial Board constituted for the campus.

The Office functions under Ordinance 3: Maintenance of Discipline Among Students, which sets out the disciplinary powers and procedure applicable across the University, including the East Delhi Campus.
